The four Logic Colours

Red · ConditionalsIf this happens, do that. Else do something else. If it rains, carry umbrella.
Green · Loops & TimersRepeat, forever, wait, countdown. The generator that keeps humming.
Purple · EventsWhen something happens, this happens. When the bell rings, line up.
