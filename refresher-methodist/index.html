Methodist High School: Monday Refresher & Judges' Questions

3 Scratch games · Olusoji Daniel Ayoola

The 45-minute refresher (one team at a time)

The game open in Scratch; the child holds the mouse.

Time What How
5 min Intro round Each child says the 4-part intro: the problem · what it is · what you do · the lesson.
15 min Code walk For each idea (events, variables, if and else, loops and timers) ask the "Show me" question below. The child points at the block and explains in one sentence.
10 min Live change Children guess first. One child makes the change, plays it, and changes it back.
10 min Full talk Run the showcase talk once, timed. Fix only one thing per child.
5 min Judges' questions Ask 3 questions from the last section.

Coaching rules: praise first · one fix per child · if a child freezes, say "Show me on the screen" · slow down, look up, point at the screen.

On the day, have ready: the .sb3 files on a flash drive · Scratch open offline if possible · the recordings on a phone as a backup.

"Show me" checks

Ask the question, the child points at the block and answers. The right column is a good answer.

Honest Scale · lesson: Honest weight, happy customers.

Show me… Good answer
**Intro:**what is it about? Some sellers give less than the customer paid for. You are Mama T selling rice; you can be honest or cheat, but cheating loses trust.
**Events:**what happens when you press SELL? _when this sprite clicked_on the Sell Button; it broadcasts_cheated_or_honest_and the customer reacts.
**Variables:**which boxes? Customer, Wanted, Kilos, Money, Trust (starts at 5). The customer pays Wanted ×₦500.
**If and else:**the big question If Kilos is less than Wanted, we cheated:₦500 extra, Trust −2. Else honest: Trust +1.
Loops and timers repeat until Customer = 6 or Trust < 1;wait until Sold = 1;_forever_keeps the scale showing Kilos.
Live change Trust −2 becomes −5: one cheat closes the shop.

Real vs Fake · lesson: Check before you buy.

Show me… Good answer
**Intro:**what is it about? Fake products are sold every day. Each round shows two look-alike products; pick the fake and a clue card shows how to tell.
**Events:**what starts each round? The Stage broadcasts_new round_; clicking a product broadcasts_correct_or_wrong_, and Oga Sharp reacts.
**Variables:**which boxes? Round, Score, Hearts (starts at 3), Fake Side (1 is left, 2 is right).
**If and else:**how does a product know it is fake? If Fake Side = 1, the left product wears the fake picture; else the real one.
Loops and timers repeat until Round = 8 or Hearts = 0;wait until Picked = 1; the clue stays 3 seconds.
Live change Hearts 3 becomes 1: one mistake and the fakes win.

Family Job Board · lesson: Help at home, every day.

Show me… Good answer
**Intro:**what is it about? Every home has chores. Mum calls a chore; you send Tayo to the right spot before Daddy comes home; more chores, better medal.
**Events:**how does a spot know Mum called it? Mum broadcasts_chore called_; every spot hears it, but only the right one glows.
**Variables:**which boxes? Time Left (45), Chores Done, Chore (which job Mum picked, 1 to 6).
**If and else:**the right spot If Chore = my number, glow; else stay plain. On a click: right spot counts, wrong spot makes Mum complain.
Loops and timers _repeat 45_with_wait 1_is the whole day; at 30 it is afternoon, at 15 evening.
Live change pick random 1 to 6 becomes 1 to 2: only 2 spots ever glow.

Likely judges' questions

Questions any team can get (with an answer starter)

Question Answer starter
What problem does your game solve? "Many people in Nigeria… Our game helps by…"
Who is it for? "Children and families who…"
Which part didyoubuild or change? "I worked on the … I changed … and tested it." (Point at it.)
What is a variable? Show me one. _"A box that remembers a number. In our game, ____ _remembers __."
Show me an if and else. _"If ___, then ___. Else, __."
What happens if you change this number? Do the live change: guess, change, play, change back.
What went wrong, and how did you fix it? _"At first __. We found it by testing, and fixed it by ___."
What would you add next? Say the family idea:"In the future, we want to add ____. This idea came from my __."
Did you build it all yourselves? "Our trainer prepared the starter. We learned how every part works, we tested it, and we changed it ourselves."
A question you cannot answer _"Good question. In our game, __. We would like _to test that next."_Then pass to a teammate.

Questions for each game

Game Likely question Good answer
Honest Scale Cheating gives more money. Why not cheat? ₦500 extra today, but trust drops by 2. If trust falls below 1, the shop closes: rich today, empty tomorrow.
Honest Scale How does the scale show the right picture? Forever, it joins the letter k with the Kilos number: k3 is the picture for 3 kilos.
Honest Scale Why does each customer want a different amount? _pick random 1 to 5_chooses the kilos, so every day is different.
Real vs Fake How does the game hide the fake? pick random 1 to 2: 1 is left, 2 is right. Even we cannot know where it will be.
Real vs Fake Why can you only answer once a round? if Picked = 0: after one click, Picked becomes 1, so more clicks do nothing.
Real vs Fake How would a real shopper check? Look closely at the packet, the seal and the NAFDAC number, and buy from trusted sellers. The clue cards teach this.
Family Job Board What happens if you click the wrong spot? The spot broadcasts_wrong_, Mum says "Not that one, Tayo!", and no chore is counted.
Family Job Board What went wrong while building? Sometimes the wrong spot glowed, because the spots checked before Mum had picked. We fixed the order: Mum picks the chore first, then broadcasts_chore called_.
Family Job Board How many chores win Gold? More than 19. More than 11 is Silver; anything less is Bronze.
