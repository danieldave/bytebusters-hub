Trainer notes: 9 build-along games

One screen per game. Read the yellow question to open, follow the build order, explain the green box, avoid the red box, and run the blue test at the end.

Naira WiseRace Against RotOwambe BudgetReal vs FakeHonest Scale (simple)Family Job BoardMiddleman MazeShrinking NairaThe Empty Barn

Say this in every game

  1. Upload and rename every sprite and backdrop first, and say each one's job.
  2. Green flag: every sprite hides.
  3. The Stage sets the rules (the boxes), then shouts "show title screen".
  4. Only the Title and PLAY listen. PLAY shouts the first message, and they both hide.
  5. Each message calls the next part of the game. Test after every step.

If nothing happens

  1. Message name different? Pick it from the dropdown, never type it twice.
  2. Sprite missing? Check its "when I receive" script and its x and y.
  3. Game stops halfway? The teller forgot to broadcast the next message.
  4. Number wrong? Check the Stage "set" block and the minus sign.

Words that keep you calm

  • "Good. Let us find out why." when something breaks.
  • "Which sprite should talk next?" before every broadcast.
  • "Read me the next block." to hand them control.
  • "Click the green flag. Let us test." after every sprite.

Naira Wise

Ada gets ₦1,000 a week for 3 weeks. 10 seconds to give each ₦200 note a job: save, spend or give. Week 2: the sandal breaks.

Open the call with

"What did you do with the last money someone gave you? Is any of it still left?"

The boxes (the Stage sets them)

Week 0

Time 10

Pocket ₦ 0

Kolo ₦ 0

Goal ₦ 1000

Goal ₦ never changes. Only the Save Jar adds to Kolo ₦. The cobbler takes ₦400 from the kolo (or the pocket, if the kolo is short).

Upload in this order
  1. Title
  2. Play Button
  3. Week Banner
  4. Mum
  5. Ada
  6. Emergency
  7. Cobbler
  8. Time Bar
  9. Save Jar
  10. Spend Jar
  11. Give Jar
  12. Leak Note
  13. Storybook
The message chain
  1. PLAY
  2. new week starts
  3. mum gives pocket money
  4. ada checks the week
  5. week 2 only: sandal breaks
  6. jars and 10 second clock start
  7. TAP
  8. time is up
  9. leftover money disappears (if money had no job)
  10. check if 3 weeks are done
  11. new week starts (again) or ada checks her kolo
  12. buy the storybook (if Kolo reached Goal)
The one thing to explain

The Week Banner shouts "new week starts" again until Week is 3. That is a loop made of messages, with no repeat block.

Watch out

Each jar checks "is Pocket ₦ > 0?" before it takes a note. Without that check, Ada can spend money she does not have.

Test before you say "done"

Save 3 notes every week: Kolo ₦ reaches 1,400, Ada buys the book, 400 is left. Save nothing in week 1: in week 2 only ₦600 is left to share.

The build-along steps (on the website)
  1. Upload and rename
  2. Green flag: every sprite hides
  3. The Stage sets the rules and shouts
  4. Only the Title and PLAY listen
  5. A new week starts
  6. Mum gives the pocket money
  7. Ada checks the week
  8. Week 2: the sandal breaks
  9. The 10-second clock and the three jars
  10. Time is up: does any money have no job?
  11. Another week, or the end?
  12. The end: can Ada buy the storybook?

Race Against Rot

100 tomatoes and an 8-second trip. Choose open air, clay pot or sun-drying. The buyer pays only for fresh tomatoes.

Open the call with

"In tomato season, why do we still see so many rotten tomatoes in the market?"

The boxes (the Stage sets them)

Time 8

Tomatoes 100

Rot 0

Price ₦ 0

Money ₦ 0

A card puts its own numbers in Rot and Price ₦. The Trip Map takes away Rot tomatoes every second. The Buyer adds Price ₦ to Money ₦ once for each fresh tomato.

Upload in this order
  1. Title
  2. Play Button
  3. Farmer
  4. Open Air Card
  5. Clay Pot Card
  6. Sun-Dry Card
  7. Truck
  8. Trip Map
  9. Buyer
The message chain
  1. PLAY
  2. farmer explains
  3. show storage cards
  4. TAP
  5. drive to market
  6. buyer pays
  7. farmer checks the money
The one thing to explain

A repeat inside a repeat: the big repeat counts 8 seconds, the small one takes away Rot tomatoes each second.

Watch out

Each card checks "is Rot = 0?" first, so only one card can be chosen. Rot must start at 0 on the Stage.

Test before you say "done"

Open air: 36 tomatoes, Money ₦ 3600. Clay pot: 76, 7600 (best). Sun-dry: 92, but ₦50 each: 4600.

Honest Scale (simple)

3 customers each want 3 kilos. Click FULL WEIGHT or LESS WEIGHT. After 3 customers, Mama T checks Trust. Only 6 sprites.

Open the call with

"Has a seller ever given you less than you paid for? Did you go back to that seller?"

The boxes (the Stage sets them)

Trust 5

Customer 1

Honest adds 1 to Trust, cheating takes away 2. Mama T adds 1 to Customer after each sale.

Upload in this order
  1. Title
  2. Play Button
  3. Mama T
  4. Customer
  5. Full Scale
  6. Less Scale
The message chain
  1. PLAY
  2. customer comes
  3. TAP
  4. honest sale / cheating sale
  5. next customer
  6. customer comes (again) or shop closes
The one thing to explain

One message, "customer comes", brings out Mama T, the customer and both scales. Mama T sends it again for the next customer, so we never copy sprites.

Watch out

Both scales must listen for BOTH "honest sale" and "cheating sale" and hide, or the old scale stays on screen.

Test before you say "done"

Honest with all 3: Trust 8, Mama T happy. Cheat once: Trust 5, Mama T worried. Cheat every time: Trust -1.

The build-along steps (on the website)
  1. Upload and rename
  2. Green flag: every sprite hides
  3. The Stage sets the rules and shouts
  4. Only the Title and PLAY listen
  5. "customer comes": everyone comes out
  6. Click a scale
  7. The customer reacts
  8. Mama T counts the customers
  9. The shop closes

The Empty Barn

6 animals. At a surprise time one gets a little sick. Click it fast. If you wait, it gets very sick, then it is lost. Keep the barn full until sunset.

Open the call with

"How can you tell when a goat or chicken is starting to get sick?"

The boxes (the Stage sets them)

Time 45

Score 0

Animals Left 6

Vaccines Left 3

Costume number tells how sick an animal is: 1 healthy, 2 a little sick, 3 very sick. A lost animal takes 1 from Animals Left.

  2. Play Button
  3. Farmer
  4. Farmhand

  7. Sheep
  8. Duck

The message chain
  1. PLAY
  2. farmer explains
  3. farm day starts
  4. TAP sick animals
  5. vaccine heals the sick (side job)
  6. sunset comes
  7. farmer gives the result
The one thing to explain

Each animal waits "pick random 4 to 12" seconds, so sickness comes at a surprise time, like on a real farm.

Watch out

At sunset each animal must "stop other scripts in sprite", or animals keep getting sick after the day ends.

Test before you say "done"

Treat every animal early: Animals Left 6, the farm is strong. Never click: animals are lost one by one.
