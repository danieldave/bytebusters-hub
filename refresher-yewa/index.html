The 45-minute refresher (one team at a time)

The project open on the screen; the child holds the mouse.

Time What How
5 min Intro round Each child says the 4-part intro: the problem · what it is · what you do · the lesson.
15 min Code walk For each idea (events, variables, if and else, loops and timers) ask the "Show me" question below. The child points and explains in one sentence.
10 min Live change Children guess first. One child makes the change, plays it, and changes it back.
10 min Full talk Run the showcase talk once, timed. Fix only one thing per child.
5 min Judges' questions Ask 3 questions from the last section.

Coaching rules: praise first · one fix per child · if a child freezes, say "Show me on the screen" · slow down, look up, point at the screen.

On the day, have ready: the project files on a flash drive · the recordings on a phone (play them if the laptop or internet fails) · AI apps tested on the venue Wi-Fi, with a recording as backup.

"Show me" checks: the 3 Scratch games

Ask the question, the child points at the block and answers. The right column is a good answer.

Naira Wise · lesson: Save some, spend some, give some.

Show me… Good answer
**Intro:**what is it about? Pocket money disappears. Ada gets₦1,000 a week and splits it into SAVE, SPEND and GIVE jars to buy a₦1,000 storybook.
**Events:**what starts each week? PLAY broadcasts_start game_; each week the Stage broadcasts_pocket money_and Ada answers.
**Variables:**which boxes? Week, Pocket, Kolo. One SAVE click: Pocket −200, Kolo +200.
**If and else:**the sandal If Kolo is less than₦400, the money comes from this week's pocket; else the kolo pays.
Loops and timers _repeat 3_plays the week 3 times;wait until Pocket = _0_waits for the player.
Live change Mum's₦1,000 becomes₦2,000: Ada needs 10 clicks a week.

Race Against Rot · lesson: Store it well, sell it fresh.

Show me… Good answer
**Intro:**what is it about? Tomatoes spoil before market. You choose open air, clay pot or sun-drying, and see how many stay fresh and how much the farmer earns.
**Events:**what happens when you pick a card? when this sprite clicked_on the card; the Stage then broadcasts_drive_and_sell.
**Variables:**which boxes? Tomatoes, Rot Per Second, Price, Money. Money = Tomatoes × Price.
**If and else:**the farmer's face If Rot Per Second = 3 (clay pot), he smiles; else he says try the clay pot next time.
Loops and timers repeat 8_with_wait 1: 8 seconds on the road, tomatoes minus rot each second.
Live change Clay pot rot 3 becomes 6: 52 tomatoes,₦5,200.

The Great Owambe Budget · lesson: Enjoy today, but plan for Monday.

Show me… Good answer
**Intro:**what is it about? Parties can leave debt. You have₦200,000 to choose jollof, canopy, DJ and more; borrowing helps today but costs more on Monday.
**Events:**what happens on PARTY TIME? It broadcasts_party_; the Stage, caption and host react.
**Variables:**which boxes? Budget, Debt, Joy. Buying jollof: Budget −80,000, Joy +1. Borrowing: Budget +50,000, Debt +60,000.
**If and else:**buying a card If Budget is less than the price,too expensive; else buy it.
Loops and timers No repeat:_wait 4_after the party, then if Debt > 0, Monday comes.
Live change Debt 60,000 becomes 100,000: the lender asks for ₦100,000.

Inflation Explainer AI · lesson: Know why prices rise, and plan how you spend.

Show me… Good answer
Intro Prices keep rising. You type an item, old and new prices and years; our code works out the %, the AI explains it simply.
Events _form.addEventListener("submit")_runs when you click Explain it;_fetch_sends the order to the server.
Variables _const order_holds what was typed; our own maths gives 16.7% for₦300 →₦350.
If and else If the new year is before the old year, stop before calling the AI.
Loops and safety A loop checks all 4 numbers; the prompt says use our % exactly; the key stays on the server.

Community Voice AI · lesson: Speak up politely, and get things done.

Show me… Good answer
Intro Young people see problems but don't know how to write a polite letter. You pick the reader and tone, describe the problem, and the AI writes a first draft.
Events Submit writes the letter; the Copy button has its own click event.
Variables The order holds reader, tone, problem, wish; the letter goes into an editable box.
If and else If the problem is under 5 or over 400 letters, or the tone is not one of ours, stop.
Loops and safety A 1.5-second timer resets Copy; the prompt says never invent names, be polite, tell a trusted adult if there is danger.
Try to break it Type only_tap_:Describe the problem in 5 to 400 letters.

Naija Career Compass AI · lesson: Many routes, one future: yours.

Scam Shield · lesson: Think before you click.

Show me… Good answer
Intro Scam messages reach phones every day. Learn the scam types, spot red flags, check a message, ask questions, take a quiz; works with no data.
Events Clicking a menu link (hashchange) shows that page; the Check button runs_verify_.
Variables All lists live in data.js;_let points_is remembered on the device.
If and else 6 risk points or more: HIGH; 3 or more: MEDIUM; else LOW.
Loops and timers A loop checks every red flag and adds up their weight; a timer hides the message after 2.6 s.
Live change First badge 30 → 10 points: confetti after one right answer.

Harvest Calendar Naija · lesson: Plant on time, harvest more.

Show me… Good answer
Intro Farmers need the right month. Pick a month; see what to plant and harvest, with a tip per crop.
Events Changing the month redraws everything; clicking a crop shows its tip; Play the year starts the timer.
Variables _const crops_lists 6 crops with their months; _currentMonth_adds 1 because JavaScript counts January as 0.
If and else Both planting and harvest: half and half; only planting: green; only harvest: gold.
Loops and timers A loop inside a loop: 6 crops × 12 months = 72 boxes;_setInterval_every 1.2 s.
Live change 1200 → 300: the year plays in about 4 seconds.

Apprentice Pathway Explorer · lesson: Learn a trade, build a future.

Show me… Good answer
Intro Not every career starts at university. 6 trades; search or pick a group; each card shows skills, training and a first step.
Events Click a card to open it; group buttons filter; typing in search redraws the cards.
Variables _const trades_lists 6 trades;_activeGroup_remembers the chosen group.
If and else If the trade is not in the chosen group, or the word is not in it, skip it; nothing shown means_No trade_ found.
Loops _for (const trade of trades)_builds one card per trade.
Live change activeGroup "All" → "Food": the page opens on Food only.

Likely judges' questions

Questions any team can get (with an answer starter)

Question Answer starter
What problem does your project solve? "Many people in Nigeria… Our project helps by…"
Who is it for? "Children and families who…"
Which part didyoubuild or change? "I worked on the … I changed … and tested it." (Point at it.)
What is a variable? Show me one. "A box that remembers a number. In our project, ____ remembers __."
Show me an if and else. _"If ___, then ___. Else, __."
What happens if you change this number? Do the live change: guess, change, play, change back.
What went wrong, and how did you fix it? _"At first __. We found it by testing, and fixed it by ___."
What would you add next? Say the family idea:"In the future, we want to add ____. This idea came from my __."
Did you build it all yourselves? "Our trainer prepared the starter. We learned how every part works, we tested it, and we changed it ourselves."
A question you cannot answer _"Good question. In our project, __. We would like _to test that next."_Then pass to a teammate.
