Unity High School: Monday Refresher & Judges' Questions

3 Scratch games · Olusoji Daniel Ayoola

The 45-minute refresher (one team at a time)

The game open in Scratch; the child holds the mouse. Signing pairs practise together: one signs, the other points at the screen.

Time What How
5 min Intro round Each child says (or signs) the 4-part intro: the problem · what it is · what you do · the lesson.
15 min Code walk For each idea (events, variables, if and else, loops and timers) ask the "Show me" question below. The child points at the block and explains in one sentence.
10 min Live change Children guess first. One child makes the change, plays it, and changes it back.
10 min Full talk Run the showcase talk once, timed. Pause after each paragraph for signers. Fix only one thing per child.
5 min Judges' questions Ask 3 questions from the last section.

Coaching rules: praise first · one fix per child · if a child freezes, say "Show me on the screen" · slow down, look up, point at the screen.

On the day, have ready: the .sb3 files on a flash drive · Scratch open offline if possible · the recordings on a phone as a backup · Shrinking Naira: press f to freeze the screen when signers need more time.

"Show me" checks

Ask the question, the child points at the block and answers. The right column is a good answer.

Middleman Maze · lesson: Work together, earn together.

Show me… Good answer
**Intro:**what is it about? Farmers sell cheaply but buyers pay much more. You drive the farmer's truck past 3 middlemen, then try the farmers' co-op road.
**Events:**what happens at each stop? The truck broadcasts_gate 1_,gate 2,_gate 3_and waits; each middleman hears his message and takes his cut.
**Variables:**which boxes? Trip, Price, Farmer Gets. Each cut is added to Price only; Farmer Gets stays at₦10,000.
**If and else:**the farmer's face If Trip = 1, he looks sad; else (trip 2, the co-op) he is happy.
Loops and timers wait until key space pressed, then glide to the next gate; the Stage uses_broadcast and wait_so trip 2 starts after trip 1.
Live change Co-op Farmer Gets 20,000 becomes 22,000.

Shrinking Naira · lesson: Plan first, then buy.

Show me… Good answer
**Intro:**what is it about? The same money buys less food every month: inflation. Mum has₦5,000 a month for 5 foods while prices rise.
**Events:**what starts a new month? The NEXT MONTH button broadcasts_new month_; Iya Oja hears it and apologises; the f key freezes the game.
**Variables:**which boxes? Month, Money, and one box for each price. Every month Money resets to₦5,000 but rice goes up by 300, bread by 200.
**If and else:**buying If Money is less than the price,too expensive; else Mum buys it.
Loops and timers No repeat block: the NEXT MONTH button is our loop, repeating the month with higher prices.
Live change Rice +300 becomes +1000: rice is₦2,800 in month 2.

The Empty Barn · lesson: Catch it early, save the farm.

Show me… Good answer
**Intro:**what is it about? Animals are lost when sickness is not noticed early. Click a sick animal fast to treat it; 3 vaccines protect all at once.
**Events:**how does one vaccine reach every animal? The Vaccine Button broadcasts_vaccinate_; all 6 animals hear it at the same time.
**Variables:**which boxes? Score, Animals Left (6), Vaccines Left (3), Time Left (45). Early treatment +20, late +10.
**If and else:**treating an animal If its costume shows early signs, heal it for 20; if very sick, heal it for 10;_not_healthy means the vaccine heals it.
Loops and timers Each animal:repeat until Time Left = 0, wait a random 4 to 12 seconds, then_wait 3_twice before it is lost.
Live change Vaccines 3 becomes 10: almost no animal is lost.

Likely judges' questions

Questions any team can get (with an answer starter)

Question Answer starter
What problem does your game solve? "Many people in Nigeria… Our game helps by…"
Who is it for? "Children and families who…"
Which part didyoubuild or change? "I worked on the … I changed … and tested it."(Point at it.)
What is a variable? Show me one. "A box that remembers a number. In our _game, ___ remembers __."
Show me an if and else. _"If ___, then ___. Else, __."
What happens if you change this number? Do the live change: guess, change, play, change back.
What went wrong, and how did you fix it? _"At first __. We found it by testing, and fixed _it by __."
What would you add next? Say the family idea:"In the future, we want _to add ___. This idea came from my __."
Did you build it all yourselves? "Our trainer prepared the starter. We learned how every part works, we tested it, and we changed it ourselves."
How did you make it work for Deaf and hard- of-hearing players? "The boxes and speech are always on screen, and in Shrinking Naira the f key freezes the game so signers have time."
A question you cannot answer _"Good question. In our game, __. We would _like to test that next."_Then pass to a teammate.
