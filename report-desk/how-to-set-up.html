BYTEBUSTERS REPORT DESK · VERSION 1

Put the Report Desk online

About 15 minutes, on a laptop. You need your Gmail account and a Netlify account.

What is in this folder

FileWhat it does
upload-to-netlify/index.htmlThe Report Desk page. This is what your team opens on their phones.
google-sheet-script/Code.gsThe script that turns a Google Sheet into the desk's storage and sends reports to your Gmail.
HOW-TO-SET-UP.htmlThis guide.

Part A Make the Google Sheet

About 10 minutes. Do this once.

  1. Sign in to the Gmail account that should own all the records. Open sheets.new in the browser. A new, empty Google Sheet opens. Name it ByteBusters Report Desk (click "Untitled spreadsheet" at the top left).
  2. Click Extensions then Apps Script. A new tab opens with a file called Code.gs.
  3. Select everything in that file and delete it. Open google-sheet-script/Code.gs from this folder in Notepad, copy all of it, and paste it in. Click the Save icon (the floppy disk).
  4. Click the ⚙ Project Settings gear on the left. Under Time zone, choose (GMT+01:00) Lagos. Then click <> Editor to go back.
  5. At the top, in the box next to ▷ Run, choose setup. Click ▷ Run.
    Google asks for permission. Click Review permissions, choose your Gmail account, then Advanced and Go to ByteBusters Report Desk (unsafe), then Allow. Google shows this warning for every script you write yourself; this one only touches this Sheet and sends email from your account.
  6. Go back to the Sheet tab. You now have four tabs: Settings, Sessions, Classes and Curriculum (all 35 rows of the 4-year curriculum). Open Settings and write down:
    • teamPin: the PIN for trainers and support staff (4 digits).
    • adminPin: your own PIN (6 digits). Never share it.
    • Check reportEmail is your Gmail. To send reports to more than one person, separate the addresses with commas.
  7. Optional check: back in Apps Script, choose sendTestEmail and click ▷ Run. A test email arrives in your Gmail within a minute.
  8. Click the blue Deploy button, then New deployment. Click the gear next to "Select type" and choose Web app. Set:
    • Description: Report Desk v1
    • Execute as: Me
    • Who has access: Anyone
    Click Deploy. Copy the Web app URL. It ends in /exec.
    "Anyone" only means the page can reach the Sheet. Nobody can read or add anything without the team PIN.

Part B Connect the page

About 2 minutes.

  1. Right-click upload-to-netlify/index.html, choose Open with then Notepad.
  2. Near the top, find this line:
    <script>window.RD_API = "";</script>
    Paste your Web app URL between the two quotes, so it looks like:
    <script>window.RD_API = "https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfy.../exec";</script>
    Save the file.
  3. Test it: double-click index.html to open it in your browser. Type your adminPin, log one test session, and check your Gmail for the "Session logged" email. Delete the test session afterwards (you can, because you used the admin PIN).

Part C Put it on Netlify

About 2 minutes.

  1. Open app.netlify.com/drop and sign in.
  2. Drag the whole upload-to-netlify folder onto the page. Netlify gives you a link straight away.
  3. Optional: in Netlify, open Site configuration then Change site name, and choose something like bytebusters-report-desk. Your link becomes bytebusters-report-desk.netlify.app.
  4. Send your team the link and the team PIN on WhatsApp. They open it on their phones, type the PIN once, and the phone remembers it.

How it works every day

WhoWhat they do
Trainers and support staff (team PIN)Open the link after each class, tap Log a session, fill it in, tap Save. They can view reports, download Word files and edit sessions.
You (admin PIN)Everything the team can do, plus: delete sessions, change classes, change the curriculum, and change report details. Team members can also unlock editing with your PIN in Centres & setup.
Your GmailAn email for every logged session (switch this off in Centres & setup if it is too many), plus the weekly report every Friday at 6pm. Use Email this report on the Reports tab to send any other report to yourself.
Your Google SheetEvery session is one row in the Sessions tab. It is your permanent record, and you can filter it or print it.

Good to know

  • Change a PIN: type a new value in the Settings tab of the Sheet. It works straight away. Everyone with the old PIN is asked for the new one.
  • Change the weekly email day or time: edit weeklyDay and weeklyHour in Settings, then in Apps Script run installWeeklyEmail once.
  • If you ever change Code.gs: Deploy, then Manage deployments, the pencil icon, Version: New version, then Deploy. The link stays the same.
  • Email limit: a free Gmail account can send about 100 emails a day from a script. That is plenty for a few sessions a day plus reports.
  • Children's privacy: use first names only, or no names. Reports are about the class, not one child.
Keep the admin PIN private. Anyone with it can delete sessions and change the curriculum.
